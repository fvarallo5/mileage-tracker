How it works

Three steps from first drive to a clean year-end log.

Track

Turn on auto-detect or start a trip. TrekTrack measures GPS miles and can show a Live Activity on the lock screen.

Classify

Swipe business or personal. Import gig earnings, log field costs, and keep receipts next to the trip.

Export

At tax time, share a branded workbook with your CPA — mileage, costs, and earnings in one spreadsheet (Pro).
